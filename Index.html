The following text comes from original and written answers from high school students in Lyceum Southern Luzon Inc. These chosen individuals shared their perspectives on the opportunities and challenges of ChatGPT through open-ended written questionnaires. This is an output for a research project called "CHALLENGES AND OPPORTUNITIES ENCOUNTERED BY HIGH SCHOOL STUDENTS IN USING CHATGPT AS GENERATIVE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOOL ."

(1.1) The academic opportunities gained by the students when using ChatGPT:

-What are the academic opportunities that the respondents gain in using ChatGPT?

"By giving an answer that is accurate and understandable."(Student 2)

"From a simple computer website or mobile application. I can access ChatGPT at all times from anywhere I like."(Student 3)

"It processes information very fast, saving me time when doing research."(Student 5)

"Providing information and answers that can help the students."(Student 7)

"The ability to get instant help with questions and generating study material." (Student 9)

"ChatGPT can help me research some key points or supporting details I may need to employ in my essay."(Student 3)

"It helps me save time, organize ideas, and finish tasks faster."(Student 5)

"It helps me summarize the topics and make my explanation better."(Student 6)

"It provides clear explanations and structured ideas."(Student 7)

(1.2)The academic challenges faced by the students when using ChatGPT:

-What are the academic challenges that the respondents encounter in using ChatGPT?

"My information and other things that are private."(Student 1)

"My personal details such as my contact, address, etc." (Student 7)

"Personal details that could compromise privacy."(Student 9)

"By using ChatGPT as a tool for inspiration, drafting, and editing, rather than as the sole source of content."(Student 1)

"It makes ourselves be dependent to ChatGPT."(Student 2)

"Because sometimes, ChatGPT gives wrong answers. It affects my scores."(Student 6)

"I write answers in my own words and use ChatGPT only as guide."(Student 8)

"They force me to double check whether any answer by ChatGPT is credible."(Student 9)

(1.3) Output proposed by the respondents:

-Most of them agreed to using it wisely, avoiding dependency.

"Using it wisely and avoid giving some info."(Student 2)

"ChatGPT should be limited only as a mean of assisting/hastening the completion of academic tasks."(Student 3)

"Just use ChatGPT as a basis and don't be dependent on it."(Student 7)

"Focus on critical thinking and proper citation."(Student 9)
